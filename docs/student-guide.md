# Student Guide: Sharing Your Advert on the Big Screen

This guide shows you how to use the **Student Portal**. You can use it on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

You will:

1. Sign in.
2. Upload a picture of your advert.
3. Wait for an adult to check it.
4. See it on the big screen.

---

## 1. Signing in

Your teacher will give you a **username** and a **password**. Keep them to yourself and your team.

1. Open the address you've been given.
2. Tap **Student Portal**.
3. In the **Your Name** box, type the username your teacher gave you.
4. In the **Event Code** box, type the password your teacher gave you.
5. Tap **Enter Portal**.

If it works, you will see **Welcome back!** and the upload page opens.

**Good to know:**

- Your username does not care about capital letters. Your password does. Type it exactly.
- If you see **Login failed**, check your spelling and try again.

### If you get the password wrong 5 times

After **5 wrong passwords in a row**, your account is **locked for 15 minutes**. You will see a message saying there were too many failed attempts.

- Wait 15 minutes, then try again. Or
- Ask a member of staff. They can unlock it straight away.

If a message says your account **is locked**, a member of staff has locked it. Ask them for help.

### If you get sent back to the start page

You stay signed in for about an hour. After that, the site sends you back to the start page. Just sign in again.

---

## 2. Uploading your advert

1. Tap the big box that says **Drop your story here**. (On a computer you can also drag your picture into it.)
2. Choose your picture.
3. Wait while the page checks your picture. It shows **Checking image…**. The first check can take a little while.
4. When you see **Looks good**, you are ready.
5. Choose your **Priority** and **Duration** (see below).
6. Tap **Send it**.

When it works you will see **Sent it** and "Your story is in the queue."

To pick a different picture before sending, tap the **X** on the preview.

### Priority and Duration

- **Priority** goes from 1 (Low) to 4 (High). Higher priority shows more often on the big screen.
- **Duration** is how long your advert stays on screen each time: 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
- The page shows a **Total Cost** for your choices. Ask your teacher how costs work at your event.

### What pictures are allowed

| Rule | What happens |
|---|---|
| Picture types: JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP | Other files (like videos or PDFs) will not upload. |
| Bigger than 10 MB | It will not upload. Save a smaller copy and try again. |
| Smaller than 3 MB | You get a warning: **Image may look blurry**. You can still send it. |
| Smaller than 10 KB | Blocked. The file is probably broken. |

**Note:** The tip on the upload page says 5 MB to 15 MB is best. But right now, anything over 10 MB will fail. Aim for **5 MB to 10 MB**.

### The image checker

The page checks every picture before you can send it.

- If you see **Image not accepted**, that picture can't be used. Choose a different one.
- Only send pictures that are right for a school event.
- The checker is not the only check. An adult looks at every upload too.

---

## 3. An adult must approve it

Your advert does **not** go on the big screen straight away.

A member of staff looks at every upload first. They will either **approve** it or **reject** it. Only approved adverts appear on the big screen.

---

## 4. Checking your uploads

Scroll down to **Your uploads**. It updates by itself every few seconds.

Each upload shows a label:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **NEW** | Waiting for an adult to check it. |
| **APPROVED** | An adult said yes. |
| **REJECTED** | An adult said no. Ask a member of staff if you are not sure why. |
| **On Projector** | It is in the mix on the big screen right now. |

An upload can be **APPROVED** but not **On Projector**. That means staff have hidden it for now.

You can also see the priority, duration and cost you chose.

---

## 5. Deleting your own advert

You can only delete your own uploads.

1. In **Your uploads**, find the advert.
2. Tap **Delete**.
3. Read the message. Deleting **cannot be undone**. If it is on the big screen, it will stop showing.
4. Tap **Delete permanently**. (Or tap **Keep upload** if you changed your mind.)

---

## Tips for a great advert

These tips come from the guidance on the upload page.

- **Go big and sharp.** A 4K picture (3840 × 2160 pixels) looks crisp on the big screen.
- **Use landscape (wide) pictures.** The big screen is wide. It fills the whole screen with your picture, so tall pictures get cut off at the top and bottom.
- **Keep important words away from the edges.** The edges may be cut off, and the picture slowly zooms.
- **Save as a high-quality JPEG.** Aim for 5 MB to 10 MB.
- **Ask first.** By uploading, you confirm everyone in the photo is happy to be featured.

---

## Signing out

Tap the **Logout** icon (the arrow pointing out of a door) at the top right of the page.

---

## Need help?

Ask a **member of staff**. They can:

- unlock your account,
- reset your password,
- tell you if your advert has been checked.
